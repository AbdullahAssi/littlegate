The brief

An Android parental-control app with no accounts and no cloud. It filters a child's internet on the phone and keeps every exit behind a parent PIN. Parents install it and pay for it; children live inside it. It should feel calm and trustworthy to parents, and friendly to kids, without looking like surveillance.
